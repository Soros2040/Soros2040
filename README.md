# Julius

**Quantum computing · Quantum machine learning · Research systems**

I explore how a research question becomes a model, an experiment and a usable system. Over the past year, my work has moved from adaptive portfolio policies to financial research agents and a quantum-finance research workbench.

My current priority is quantum computing and quantum machine learning. I am interested in research assistant opportunities where I can connect mathematical questions, experimental design and research software, drawing on my work in quantitative methods and agent systems. The repositories below provide methods, implementation context, reported results and the questions I want to pursue next.

## A reading route

Start with Q-Fintelligence to see how objectives, experiments and hardware evidence fit together. Read BenjaminAgent for workflow engineering, and Alex-Fin for model architecture and portfolio evaluation. Julius' future explains the decisions and experiences behind the work.

Each project provides an English and Chinese reading path. Results retain their experimental context; design, implementation and execution are attributed according to the contribution record. References and upstream software are acknowledged within each repository.

## How I work

I want to make the problem, method, comparison and next decision understandable to another person. That means mapping explanations to source, keeping experiment identity with the results, and explaining outcomes that challenge the original hypothesis. It also means being precise about my own role in collaborative work.

To discuss a project, open an Issue in its repository. For the broader direction, read [Julius' future](https://github.com/Soros2040/julius-future).

## Complete works and close reading
